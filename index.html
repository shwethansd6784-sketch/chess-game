Rules & How to Play

Official FIDE Chess Rules & Pass-and-Play Guide

🎯 Objective

The goal is to checkmate your opponent's king. This occurs when the king is in check and cannot escape attack.

👥 Two-Player Pass & Play

Both players take turns on this device. Player 1 controls White (moves first), and Player 2 controls Black. You can use Flip Board or turn on Auto-Flip on Turn so each player faces their own army!

♟️ Special Moves

  • Castling (O-O / O-O-O): Move your king two squares towards a rook; the rook hops over to the adjacent square. (Neither king nor rook must have moved yet, and squares in between must be clear and not under attack).
  • En Passant: When a pawn advances two squares from its starting position and lands adjacent to an enemy pawn, that enemy pawn can capture it diagonally on the very next move.
  • Pawn Promotion: When a pawn reaches the 8th rank, it can immediately promote to a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight!

🤝 Draw Conditions

  • Stalemate: The player to move has no legal moves and their king is NOT in check.
  • Insufficient Material: Neither player has enough pieces to force checkmate (e.g. King vs King, King & Bishop vs King).
  • Threefold Repetition: The same board position occurs 3 times.
  • 50-Move Rule: 50 consecutive moves without a pawn move or capture.
  • Mutual Agreement: Both players accept a draw via the Offer Draw button.
